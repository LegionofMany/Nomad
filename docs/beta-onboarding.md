# Nomad Wallet — Beta Onboarding Script

## Phase One · Closed Beta

Welcome to the Nomad Phase One closed beta.

This document explains **how to use Nomad safely**, **what to expect**, and **how to participate responsibly**. Please read this fully before depositing funds.

---

## 1. What This Beta Is

Nomad Phase One is a **non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet** designed for deliberate, real-world use — especially for travelers.

This beta exists to:

* Validate usability
* Identify confusion or friction
* Confirm security assumptions
* Collect calm, honest feedback

This beta is **not**:

* A finished consumer product
* A custodial service
* A trading platform
* A guarantee against loss

---

## 2. Your Responsibility (Important)

Nomad is **non-custodial**.

* You control your keys
* You control recovery
* Nomad cannot reset access
* Lost recovery materials = permanent loss

Only proceed if you understand and accept this responsibility.

---

## 3. Recommended Funding Guidelines

For the beta:

* Start with a **small amount**
* Perform a test transaction first
* Do not store life-changing funds
* Treat this as real crypto, not test tokens

**Suggested maximum for beta testing:**

* Main wallet: user discretion
* Travel Mode prefunding: keep intentionally low

---

## 4. First-Time Setup Overview

During setup, Nomad will guide you through:

1. Secure key generation
2. Clock-based daily unlock setup
3. 24-time-set recovery creation
4. Confirmation and understanding checks

Do not rush this process.

---

## 5. Clock-Based Unlock (Daily Use)

Nomad uses a **clock-style unlock** instead of a PIN or password.

* You set a specific time
* Unlocking is done in one smooth rotation
* The motion matters, not just the final position

Tips:

* Choose a time meaningful only to you
* Practice unlocking calmly
* You can change your unlock time later

Forgetting your daily unlock does **not** risk funds.

---

## 6. Recovery System (Critical)

Nomad recovery uses **24 separate time sets** created during setup.

Important rules:

* Store recovery information offline
* Do not screenshot or cloud-store it
* Nomad cannot recover this for you

Recovery is designed for **device loss**, not convenience.

---

## 7. Travel Mode & NFC Payments

Travel Mode is optional and **off by default**.

When enabled:

* You pre-fund a spending balance
* Only that balance is exposed
* NFC must be explicitly turned on
* Limits and auto-expiry apply

If your phone is lost, Travel Mode can be frozen immediately.

Your main wallet remains protected.

---

## 8. Transactions & Approvals

Before approving any transaction:

* Read the summary
* Confirm the network
* Verify the amount

Nomad will never sign transactions silently.

If something feels unclear — stop.

---

## 9. Feedback We Want

This beta succeeds if you tell us:

* Where you felt unsure
* Where you felt anxious
* Where instructions were unclear
* Where things felt calm and intuitive

Feedback about *feelings* is just as valuable as bugs.

---

## 10. What To Do If Something Goes Wrong

* Lost device: attempt recovery on a new device
* Suspected issue: freeze Travel Mode
* Confusion: stop using the app and report feedback

There is no emergency support channel — this is intentional.

---

## 11. Final Reminder

Nomad Phase One is designed to be safe, not fast.

Take your time.
Understand what you are doing.
Only proceed when you feel calm and informed.

Thank you for helping shape Nomad.
