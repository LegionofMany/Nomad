How it works

  1. Create your wallet. The app generates your 24 Time Sets and asks you to choose a password.
  2. Back up your Time Sets. Write them down and keep them somewhere safe. They are the only way to recover your wallet.
  3. Connect an app. Choose "Sign in with Arkrilium" on a partner app and approve it on your phone.
  4. Stay in control. Review and approve each payment. Lock the wallet any time.

Questions

 

Is this available with real money?

Not yet. Arkrilium is in testnet preview. Real funds will only be enabled after independent security and legal review.

What if I lose my phone?

Install Arkrilium on a new phone and recover with your 24 Time Sets and password. If you lose both the Time Sets and the password, nobody can recover the wallet, including us.

Does Arkrilium hold my crypto?

No. It is non-custodial, so the keys stay on your device.

Which coins are supported?

The preview uses Bitcoin testnet and Ethereum-compatible test networks. More will be announced before launch.
